TIMELINE • MACHINE LEARNING

THE HISTORY OF ML

From early ideas about machine intelligence to powerful systems that learn from data, machine learning has evolved through decades of research and technological progress.

1950

FOUNDATIONS OF MACHINE INTELLIGENCE

Alan Turing published Computing Machinery and Intelligence and introduced the Turing Test, helping establish important foundations for AI and machine learning research.

MILESTONE 01
1950s–1970s

EARLY LEARNING METHODS

Researchers developed ways for computers to learn from data, rules, and feedback. Decision trees became an important method for learning patterns and making predictions.

MILESTONE 02
1990s

STATISTICAL MACHINE LEARNING

Machine learning increasingly used statistics, probability, and optimization. Methods such as Support Vector Machines and Bayesian networks improved pattern recognition and prediction.

MILESTONE 03
2010s

THE DEEP LEARNING REVOLUTION

GPUs, larger datasets, and improved training methods made deep learning much more powerful. Major breakthroughs followed in image recognition, speech processing, and computer vision.

MILESTONE 04
2017–TODAY

FROM DEEP LEARNING TO GENERATIVE AI

The Transformer architecture, introduced in 2017, transformed language processing and helped lead to today's powerful AI models. Machine learning is now used across language, healthcare, assistants, image generation, and many other applications.

MILESTONE 05
END OF TIMELINE

WHERE DO WE GO NEXT?

Machine learning has evolved from early experiments into technology that powers many of the systems we use today. Understanding its history helps us see how the field may continue to develop in the future.
